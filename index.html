Common Trading Patterns

Head and Shoulders: A reversal pattern with three peaks, the middle being the highest. Indicates a trend change from bullish to bearish.
Double Top: Two consecutive peaks at roughly the same price level, signaling a potential bearish reversal.
Double Bottom: Two consecutive troughs at roughly the same price level, signaling a potential bullish reversal.
Triangle (Ascending/Descending/Symmetrical): Price consolidates in a triangle shape, often leading to a breakout.
Flag & Pennant: Short-term continuation patterns that resemble a flag or pennant after a sharp price move.
Cup and Handle: A rounded bottom followed by a small consolidation, often leading to a bullish breakout.
